# Prototype planning policy

Version: `coach-2.0`. See [COACH.md](COACH.md) for the research review, quality-session selection, readiness inputs, cycle rules, and exact power-target policy. Numeric planning parameters live in `POLICY` in `public/engine.js`; sensor processing rules live in `SENSOR_POLICY` in `public/sensors.js`. They are explicit product heuristics, **not established sports-science thresholds**. No claim of physiological effectiveness is made. A qualified cycling coach and health reviewer must assess these rules and messages before production use. The present evidence is software scenario testing only; the evidence-informed implementation still needs independent qualified review and outcome validation.

| Rule | Current behavior | Prototype rationale / limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recent baseline | Sum cycling minutes over the last 28 local days and divide by four when at least three records exist. Include empty weeks. | Avoid extrapolating only from active weeks; missing records can understate capacity. |
| First week after reported recovery | Use four weeks ending the Sunday before the rider's recovery week. For Nov 2–8 recovery, baseline ends Nov 1 and proposal starts Nov 9. | Do not misread deliberate recovery as a loss of ordinary training capacity; no automatic intensity increase. |
| Descriptive training level | Fewer than 3 baseline rides: limited records. Otherwise 3+ active weeks out of four: regular; remaining records: intermittent. Confidence in exposure increases with 6+ records across 3+ weeks. | Labels describe recording consistency only; unvalidated and not physiological fitness scores. |
| Sparse history | At most 90 min/week, limited by self-reported typical time and actual availability. Explicit zero stays zero. | Visible cautious starting assumption; not a clinical or physiological boundary. |
| Requested progression only | No automatic increase. With at least 6 rides over 3 active baseline weeks and non-new-rider experience, an explicit requested target may increase volume up to 5% before other constraints. | A prototype product safeguard, not a universally safe physiological threshold. The rider asked for 12–13 hours; the first proposal targets 12. |
| Longest session | Limited by recorded longest ride (or reported longest when history is absent), availability, and 240 min. | Avoid creating an unprecedented long ride. A 4-hour slot only fits when recent history supports it. |
| Session arithmetic | Easy sessions include 5-minute warm-up/cool-down; quality sessions include 15/10 minutes plus intervals and recoveries. | Full step arithmetic and daily limits are validated. |
| Intensity | Mostly easy riding plus conditional goal-specific tempo, sustained, or high-aerobic intervals. | See Coach policy; no inferred FTP or HR zones. |
| Spacing | Basic/sparse plans prefer a day between easy rides. A consistent rider's explicit volume target may use consecutive easy days; reported other demanding exercise days remain reserved. | Quality sessions and strength/long rides have additional spacing rules; these do not prove recovery. |
| Other exercise | Basic plans subtract its four-week average duration. For explicit progression against an established baseline, subtract only any increase over baseline other-exercise time. | Usual other exercise already coexisted with baseline riding; avoid counting it twice. Intensity and sport equivalence remain uncertain. |
| Poor recovery | Multiply riding budget by 0.6. | Makes a meaningful reduction visible; no diagnosis or readiness score. |
| Extended break | If the latest recorded ride is over 21 days ago within the history window, halve budget. Older histories use sparse-history limits. | Restart cautiously instead of resuming an old load. |
| Event | Reject past dates; reduce budget to 0.6 within 7 days; do not schedule on/after event day. | Illustrative event preparation only. No event readiness claim or automatically prescribed event ride. |
| Stale FTP | Older than 90 days: RPE only. A current supplied FTP can add reference watt ranges and threshold snapshots. | No FTP is generated or silently updated. |
| Health boundary | No new riding for acute symptoms or reported restrictions. | Stop performance advice; direct toward urgent or qualified help as appropriate. Locked conflicts block acceptance. |

Plans may leave availability unused: capacity is a limit, not a target. Goal, experience, equipment, preferred setting, other exercise, recovery, and event timing affect the proposal or its explicit limitations. Distance and terrain do not establish fitness or advanced race prescriptions. Recommendations link to source activity IDs, dates, durations, provenance, policy version, and the assumptions captured at generation time.

Only six calendar months of API activity data are collected and assessed. [Sensor evaluation](SENSORS.md) adds observed power benchmarks, HR/power coverage, recent effort against a supplied fresh FTP reference, and uncertainty to plan explanations. Collection progress is explicit. Sensor findings never silently infer thresholds, add intensity, or override recovery and availability. Six-month best efforts and recent-week training capacity remain distinct.

Completed, skipped, partial, modified, and locked sessions are preserved. Preserved planned load may exceed a reduced budget, in which case the app reports a conflict and prevents acceptance. No missed load is moved to a later day. Original workout duration and steps remain when actual activity differs. Material changes need preview and acceptance. Undo retains recorded history, past sessions, and locked future sessions; settings remain current and can be reconciled with a fresh proposal. Snapshots are copied so subsequent locks and outcomes cannot mutate earlier prescriptions.
